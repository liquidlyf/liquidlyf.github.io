# LiquidLyf

**Find blood donors near you, for free.** [বাংলা](/bn/)

LiquidLyf is a free Android app that connects blood donors with people who need blood in Bangladesh.

{% if site.released %}**[⬇ Download for Android](/download)**{% else %}**Coming soon:** the app is being tested and isn't
available to download yet ([more](/download)).{% endif %}

## How it works

1. **Ask for blood.** Post a request with the blood group, the hospital and when it's needed.
2. **Donors nearby are alerted.** LiquidLyf alerts donors with a matching blood group near the hospital
   who can donate now. If nobody responds, it widens the search step by step.
3. **A donor offers, and you talk directly.** Phone numbers are shared only between you and the donors
   who offered.

## Made to be safe

- **Your phone number is never public.** It's shared only when you offer to help, and only with that
  requester.
- **It knows when you can donate.** The app counts your rest period after each donation and reminds you
  when you can give again.
- **Never pay for blood.** Blood donation is free. LiquidLyf has report and block buttons, and
  volunteers review reports.
- **Bangla and English,** throughout the app.
- **Free, with no ads,** and open source: anyone can [read the code](https://github.com/liquidlyf/LiquidLyf).

## For blood-donor clubs and organizations

Run a blood-donor club, a hospital blood bank or an NGO? We'd like to work with you. Email
liquidlyf@gmail.com with "Partner" in the subject.

## Help build LiquidLyf

LiquidLyf is built by volunteers. Developers, translators, designers and testers are welcome: see
[how to contribute](https://github.com/liquidlyf/LiquidLyf/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md).

---

**LiquidLyf is not an emergency service.** If someone needs blood urgently, contact the hospital or a
licensed blood bank now, or call 999.
