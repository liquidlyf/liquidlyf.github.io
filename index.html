How it works

One app for both sides: people who need blood, and the donors who can give it.

Need blood?

  1. Post a request. Tap Request blood, then add the blood group, the hospital and when it’s needed.

  2. Donors nearby get a notification. Only donors with a matching blood group near the hospital, who can donate now. If nobody responds, the search widens step by step.

  3. A donor offers, and you talk directly. You see the number of each donor who offers to help. Meet at the hospital.

You can also search for donors by blood group and area in Find donors, and ask someone to donate.

Want to donate?

  1. List yourself as a donor. Add your blood group and your area. Your phone number is never shown in the list.

  2. Get a notification when someone near you needs your blood group.

  3. Offer, then donate at the hospital. Your donation goes on your profile, and the app reminds you when you can donate again.

Made to be safe

You trust us with your phone number and your blood group. Here’s how LiquidLyf protects you.

  • Your number is never public

    A requester sees a donor’s number only after the donor offers. The donor sees the request’s contact only while the offer is active.

  • Never pay for blood

    Blood donation is free. The app warns you when a request mentions money. Anyone asking for bKash, Nagad or “transport costs” is a scammer: don’t pay, and report them.

  • Donate only at a hospital

    Donate at a licensed hospital or blood transfusion centre, where the blood is screened. Never go alone to meet a stranger anywhere else.

  • Report and block

    Report fake requests and anyone asking for money. Block people you don’t want to hear from. Volunteer moderators review every report.

  • Rest between donations

    The app counts your rest period after each donation, and sends you no alerts until you can donate again.

  • Nothing hidden

    No ads, no trackers, and the code is public. The Privacy Policy explains every piece of data in plain words.

Questions and answers

Is LiquidLyf free?

Yes. LiquidLyf is free for everyone, with no ads and nothing to buy. It’s built by volunteers and runs on the free plans of Firebase and Cloudflare.

Who can see my phone number?

Nobody, publicly: your number isn’t on any list. When you post a request, only the donors who offer to help see its contact number, and only while their offer is active. When you offer, the requester sees your number. Find donors never shows anyone’s number. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store? Is the file safe?

For now, we publish LiquidLyf ourselves, on this website and on GitHub. It may come to the Play Store later. Every genuine copy is signed with the same key, and anyone can read the app’s code. Android may warn you because the app isn’t from the Play Store; that’s expected. Only download it from this website or from GitHub Releases: a copy from anywhere else may be fake.

Who can donate, and how often?

The app follows these rules for whole blood: aged 18 to 60, weighing at least 45 kg, and at least 120 days (about 4 months) since your last donation. The app counts the days for you. On the day, the doctor or the blood bank checks you and makes the final decision.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete your account. Without the app, email liquidlyf@gmail.com from your account’s email address, with “Delete my account” in the subject. All the details.

For blood-donor clubs and organizations

Run a blood-donor club, a hospital blood bank or an NGO? We’d like to work with you. Email liquidlyf@gmail.com with “Partner” in the subject.

Help build LiquidLyf

LiquidLyf is built by volunteers. Developers, translators, designers and testers are welcome: see how to contribute.
